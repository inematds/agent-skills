YOU WILL BUILD

A report that proves where the numbers came from.

The main project turns a sales CSV into a weekly report, with exact calculations, input validation, and evidence. A second case follows the visual process of transforming a video into an article.

  1. Define input, output, and acceptance criteria.
  2. Write and install the skill in the project.
  3. Run the example and reject invalid inputs.
  4. Log an improvement and its regression test.

References and editorial criteria

The six steps were adapted from the transcription provided by the video’s user How to Develop Skills in Codex Better than 99% of People. The video is the methodological reference; examples, exercises, diagrams, and the lab were developed for this course.

“Better than 99%” is the title of the source, with no statistical proof presented in this material. Names, times, and comparisons from the video are not a current table of prices nor a universal ranking of models.

Technical reference consulted in 21/09/2026: OpenAI — Build skills, for SKILL.md, scopes and triggering; OpenAI — Customization, to distinguish rules, skills, and tools. Available options depend on the version and the account.
